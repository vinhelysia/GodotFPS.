Town500m Phase1 glass vision pilot — 2026-10-08

Scope and integration
- Only Scripts/Enemies/scav_perception.gd is changed in gameplay code.
- _los_clear(from, to, target) retains its callers/API, mask3, target/group/child
  acceptance and clear empty-ray policy. It repeats the same native ray with
  excluded pane RIDs; no origin offset can tunnel through a wall behind a pane.
- Tag actual glass-only StaticBody3D with group ai_vision_transparent.
  Never tag parent/combined wall-frame bodies: Godot RID exclusion excludes all
  shapes of that body. Mesh/material appearance does not assign transparency.
- Four tagged static pane bodies may be skipped; a fifth fails closed. There are
  at most five native ray queries per aim point. Untagged bodies and tagged
  non-static bodies remain opaque. Existing ray-start-inside behavior is retained.
- Do not change native collision layers/masks. Separate pane body stays layer1;
  player collision, player damage mask3, and Scav hitscan mask19 still hit it.
- No shared Cogito/BT combat/scene/settings/asset/manager/autoload changes.
